Design a complete UI/UX ecosystem for an EdTech SaaS startup called "ExamFlow" (temporary name).

## Product Vision

Create a scalable digital examination management platform that helps educational organizations manage the entire exam lifecycle:

* Creating and organizing exams
* Managing students and teachers
* Digital grading and correction
* Transparent review of graded papers
* Automated results publishing
* Student performance analytics
* Digital appeal management

The product should be designed as a global SaaS startup, not as a government system.

Target customers:

* Private schools
* Universities
* Educational institutes
* Training centers
* Corporate training academies
* Government organizations as future enterprise customers
